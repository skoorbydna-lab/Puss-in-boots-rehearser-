WHAT I HEARD

Voice mode uses your browser's speech tools. For best results, use Chrome on a phone or computer, allow microphone access, and rehearse somewhere quiet. Speech recognition can mishear words, especially with stage names or expressive delivery, so scores are a guide rather than a judgement of acting.
